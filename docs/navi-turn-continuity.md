# Navi turn continuity and workspace worker recovery

Plan omf-navi-agent-continuity-20261022 (self-mapped FOLD). This is the current behaviour. Earlier documents keep their dated evidence; where they say "current turn only" or "assigned at send", this map supersedes them.

## What a later turn knows

- **Earlier messages.** The claimed company job's `context.messages` is rebuilt at claim from the reconciled live conversation segment, never the current turn: the last 8 earlier turns with `turnId`, `text`, `response`, `submittedAt`, `replyRecordedAt`, `replyState` (`answered`, `failed`, `not_answered`) and up to 10 receipts per turn (`actionCount`/`actionsTruncated` beyond that). A receipt is the safe `companyActionReceipt` projection: operation id, name, turn, slot, target, state, requested fields, native resource ids, completion time, recovery owner, safe error. Leases, payload hashes, markers and arguments are never projected. The send stores the same projection; the claim replaces it, so a write recovered after the send is current.
- **History facts.** `facts.conversationHistory` gives `earlierTurns`, `inContext`, `olderTurnsAvailable` and the reader name. The interpreter passes `facts` as `evidence`.
- **Current turn.** `job.actions` and `nextSlot` still come from the current-turn reader (`companyActionReceipts`), unchanged.
- **Status read.** `get_company_action_status` uses `companyConversationActionReceipts`. With an `operationId` it returns that operation from any live turn, or nothing (unknown, archived, another actor, company or conversation). Without one it returns this turn's receipts as before, plus `earlierReceipts` (newest first, at most 30, with `earlierReceiptTotal` and `earlierReceiptsTruncated`). It reconciles like the claim and never repeats an effect. The lease is checked again after the read.
- **Earlier turns.** `get_company_conversation_turns` (the 45th company tool) reads earlier turns of this live segment only: literal, case-insensitive search of the question and answer, a `beforeTurnId` cursor, `limit` 1–20 (default 10) and a page bounded to 24,000 characters. It reports `truncated` (`limit` or `size`), `hasMore` and `nextBeforeTurnId`. A cursor outside this live segment (another conversation, an archived turn, the current turn) returns `cursorFound: false` and no turns. It reads no archive; archived conversations stay with `list_company_conversation_history` and `get_company_conversation_archive`.
- **Guidance.** One company prompt paragraph: earlier messages and receipts are outcomes, never a fresh instruction; after a lost reply, report an already-completed result instead of repeating it; read a working or unresolved earlier receipt with the status tool first; a current explicit request for an additional, separate record is still carried out. No keyword filter, no confirmation step, and the model, effort and turn budget are unchanged.

## Who claims a queued turn

- **Owner workspace and company turns** are assigned at claim, the purchase chat's existing pattern. The worker recorded at send (`agentId` on the queued row) is only a hint. Any live workspace-enabled worker of the same owner claims queued work, or expired work with fewer than two attempts. The atomic claim sets `agentId` to that worker with a new lease.
- **A live lease is never taken.** Action, result, heartbeat and public-research calls still require the assigned worker and its current lease, so a replaced worker's late calls get 409.
- **Exhausted work** (expired, two attempts) and company work the owner no longer belongs to fail on any workspace claim of that owner, so nothing waits under an offline or revoked worker. The explicit retry stays the next lane.
- **Grants stay the claiming worker's.** Owner actions use the claiming worker's own `accountIds`; a narrower worker gets 404 on another account's statement. Public research is granted only from the claiming worker's fresh capability report and frozen on its lease; a reclaim without the capability carries none.
- **Unchanged:** purchase chat claims, native finance writes and their markers, the owner action journal, the company teaching bridge and fresh membership checks.

## Callers

- Upstream: `[...path].ts` company-workspace send/retry, owner workspace send/retry, worker `workspace-claim` (heartbeat with the capability report, then `claimAssistant` and `claimCompanyAssistant`).
- `companyActionReceipts`: the claim only. `companyConversationActionReceipts`: the status tool only.
- Downstream: `teaching-worker/interpreter.mjs` (`interpreterInput` passes messages and facts; the prompt names the reader). `actions-mcp.mjs` lists `job.tools` and needed no change.

## Guards for independent review

- Status and history scope: the frozen job's conversation (`_id`, owner, company), live turns only, the current turn excluded from history.
- Claim predicate: owner, allowed organizations, state, lease and attempts; assignment in the same atomic update.
- Account grants and public research bound to the claiming worker.

## Limits

- These tests prove transport, journal, claim and lease behaviour, not model judgement. The fixed actual-model corpus (lost-reply repeat versus an intentional additional record, zero-write questions, uncertainty) is later F5 evidence.
- An operation already settled `unresolved` is not reconciled again if its native write lands later (existing journal rule).
- Closed-chat status and read state, selected-record UI and deployment remain open.

## Owner workspace and purchase request identity across New chat

Plan `output/plan/omf-navi-agent-owner-retry-20261023` (WIDE). The company conversation gained request identity across New chat in plan `omf-navi-agent-pending-exit-20261023` (`docs/navi-selected-records.md`); this fold gives the owner workspace and purchase chats the same useful behaviour with their own authorities, receipts and masking. Before it, an accepted owner or purchase request id sent again after New chat was queued and claimed again: the owner journal handed the claim the earlier receipt and the next free slot (same-slot calls stayed idempotent; a controlled next slot then wrote a second synthetic intake record), and a purchase explanation was re-queued. These were controlled synthetic sequences, never a model decision, a payment or a production duplicate.

### Server

- **Reader.** `findOwnerReceivedRequest(kind, identity, requestId, live, organizationId)` (`server/services/financeOwnerConversationService.ts`, exported; importers `financeAssistantService.ts` and `financeChatService.ts`; New chat uses it internally) returns `null` or `{ turn, answer }`. It reads the live turn first (with its journal receipts), then every **adopted** archive of exactly this conversation (`ownerId`, `conversationKind`, the live row's `conversationId`, `segment` below the live segment), newest first, at most 10, with `occurrences`/`occurrencesTruncated` for a request accepted more than once before this fold (a request found in more than 10 adopted archives is reported as 10 with `occurrencesTruncated: true`; History states that as the lower bound 「10件以上」, never as an exact count). An unadopted reset copy and another owner's, realm's, purchase's or conversation's archive are never read. `answer` is `{ requestId, outcome: 'accepted', where: 'live' | 'archive', scope: { actorId, realm: 'workspace' | 'purchase', conversationId, organizationId (the checked company it was masked for, or null), importId and line for a purchase }, turn: { id, at, answeredAt, actions, text and replyState, or withheld }, archive?, occurrences?, occurrencesTruncated? }`. The turn is masked exactly as History shows it: a turn that used another company's teachings is withheld (no text, reply or teaching receipt). The request hash, frozen company, references and context never leave the server.
- **Meaning.** A new turn stores `requestHash`: owner workspace `sha256(canonicalJson({ text, context }))` of its trimmed text and the page context as sent; purchase `sha256(canonicalJson({ text, draft: { revision, key, sourceHash }, references }))` of its trimmed text, the draft binding it was sent against and the displayed references it carried. Never the conversation revision or the selected company (the turn freezes the posting company separately, and a recognised retry never moves it). `sameOwnerRequestMeaning` compares that hash exactly. **Legacy turns** stored before this fold carry no hash; they are matched by their exact trimmed text, the rule the live check always used. No turn is rewritten or migrated, and a legacy turn's context, binding or references cannot be compared (limit below).
- **One canonical id.** The existing 16-64 character check is unchanged; its string form is the one id for recognition, `currentId` and the stored turn, so an id sent as a JSON number (which already passed the rule) is claimable and is the same request as its string form. Before, such a turn kept the number while `currentId` held the string, and the owner claim failed with 500 (purchase likewise). Stored ids compare by their string form (`sameOwnerRequestId`), and the archive lookup also asks for the exact number when the id is an exact integer, so a legacy turn that kept its number is still recognised. No new rejection.
- **Send** (`sendAssistant`, `sendChat`): identity check, then (purchase) the owned draft context, the live recognition, the archived recognition, then every existing check. The same meaning returns the current view and, for an archived turn, `received` (the answer above); nothing is queued or executed. Another meaning keeps the existing 409 「送信IDが既に使用されています。」. For a purchase the recognition precedes the draft binding, editability, worker and conversation checks, so the request's own completed save (which moved the draft) never turns its retry into a refusal or a new instruction. A new id with identical words is a new request (no date, amount, name or text deduplication).
- **New chat** (`newAssistant`, `newChat` → `startNewOwnerConversation(kind, identity, body, pending = null, create = null)`): the body may name the client's uncertain earlier send (`pending: { requestId, text, context }`, or `{ requestId, text, revision, key, sourceHash, references }` for a purchase), normalised with the send's own identity rules plus the checked company it is masked for; context for the answer only. `create` is the server-checked identity a fence needs to create an absent conversation row (owner `{ ownerId }`; purchase `{ ownerId, importId, line, accountId }` of the owned draft). With a named request the answer is **accepted** (a live turn is archived by this reset and answered from that archive; or an adopted archive) or **not accepted**, only after this reset's revision-checked write advanced the conversation past every earlier attempt: the archive write on a non-empty segment, a fence on an empty segment (revision + 1, `lastReset` with the meaning, no archive and no history noise), or with no conversation the row itself created at revision 1. The response gains `reset: { requestId, scope, pending: answer }` only then. A lost reset reply is recognised only with the meaning it was first sent with (otherwise 409) and fences again before "not accepted". "Not accepted" means only that Navi never stored that request id in this conversation and no earlier attempt can be stored now; it is not a cancellation and says nothing about anything done outside Navi.
- **Unchanged without a named request:** non-empty segments are archived, empty or absent conversations stay no-ops, three-argument callers behave as before, and `lastReset` gains `pending`/`outcome`/`revision` only for a named request (a Mixed field; no schema or index change). Receipt reconciliation, the archive copy rules, archived proposals without apply references and the busy/confirming/working/stale refusals are unchanged.

### Client

- `components/finance/AssistantConversation.vue`, owner realm (the company pending protocol keeps its exact checks): New chat stays available with an uncertain request even with no turns, and names the frozen request with its frozen reset id. `settle()` applies an answer only for the same pending object, this actor, the workspace realm and the conversation shown, under the selection it was requested in. Accepted: released, its text cleared only if unchanged, History opens the archived turn framed with its receipts, and the open pages refresh from its completed receipts. Not accepted: released, the typed text kept. The owner pending request now keeps the company selection it was made under and is never sent under another (send held, with an explanation); New chat (which sends nothing) or switching back resolves it. The recovery hint shows in both realms. History's note for an accepted request found in more archives than the server reads states the capped count as a lower bound (「10件以上」, from `occurrencesTruncated`), in the owner and the company realm alike (the company answer carries the same flag); an uncapped count is shown as before.
- `components/finance/PurchaseChat.vue`: the uncertain send moved from component memory to the store's pending map (memory only): id, text, composer text, the frozen draft binding, the displayed references and the selection. Every later press sends exactly that request (an edited composer text stays for later) until an authoritative answer: the view showing its turn (id and text), `received`, or New chat's answer. No failure releases it, the first attempt's included: `sendChat` follows its single storing write with `getChat`, which re-runs `readDraft` (owned import, `assertEditableImportRow`, the source mapping and the saved-draft/source integrity checks, each able to refuse with 409), so a 400 or 409 is not proof that nothing was stored, and the purchase send carries no per-request not-stored acknowledgement (unlike the company send's marked `selectedRecordMissing` refusal). A refused, lost or failed attempt keeps the same uncertain request: 送信結果を確認 sends exactly it again (a stored one is recognised, live or archived) and New chat answers it (not accepted: released, the text kept). New chat names it with its frozen meaning and is available with no turns or no conversation; History frames an accepted archived turn and the draft page refreshes from its completed draft writes. It is held under another company selection like the owner realm. No automatic send, no confirmation step.

### Callers

- `startNewOwnerConversation` (changed signature, optional 4th and 5th arguments): `newAssistant` and `newChat` only. Exported `findOwnerReceivedRequest`, `sameOwnerRequestId`, `sameOwnerRequestMeaning`: the two services only. The route `server/api/finance-chat/[...path].ts` passes bodies unchanged (not edited). Response additions: `received` (send, archived recognition) and `reset` (New chat with a named request).

### Refusals and holds for the independent review

1. Send: the existing 409 now also covers an id accepted in an adopted archive with another meaning, and a hashed live turn sent again with another context, binding or references (before: text only). An archived same-meaning id returns `received` instead of being queued.
2. New chat with a named request: 409 「会話が更新されました。再読込してください。」 on an absent conversation with a non-zero revision, a creation racing a first send, or a lost compare-and-set not written by the same reset id; 409 「送信IDが既に使用されています。」 when a repeated reset id names another meaning (or none), or the named request was accepted with another meaning. The existing busy/confirming, still-working-receipt and stale refusals also guard the empty-segment fence and the repeated-reset fence.
3. Client holds: an uncertain owner or purchase request stays pending on every refusal (a purchase first attempt's 400/409 included), lost reply, answer about another request or answer after an actor or selection change, and is never sent under another company selection.
4. Canonical id: no new refusal; stored rows are not rewritten.

### Limits

- Controlled synthetic worker sequences and induced network losses only: transport, identity, fence, masking and client recovery, not model judgement, a production reliability rate or a payment. Rows accepted twice before this fold stay in history and are reported with their count.
- Legacy turns (no hash) are matched by exact text only. The live view still returns stored turn fields as before (a new turn's `requestHash` included); the `received`/`reset` answers never do.
- The purchase send has no per-request not-stored acknowledgement, so a definite refusal (for example a stale draft binding) also leaves the request uncertain until 送信結果を確認 or New chat settles it (one more press; the typed text is kept). The after-write 409 was probed only as induced transport evidence (the test rewrites the status the browser receives after the native server stored the send); no natural post-write refusal was reproduced and none is claimed from production.
- History's count of an accepted request is bounded by the reader (at most 10 archives); more is stated as 「10件以上」 without the exact number.
- An owner turn masked for the current selection is not released by the live view (it shows no text); New chat or switching back releases it, as before.
