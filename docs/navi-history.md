# Purchase history in Navi

`get_purchase_history` is a read in purchase chat and the workspace. It uses the existing live lease, owner and assigned-card target checks. A purchase chat stays bound to its own purchase. In the workspace, select/name a statement and provide the line.

The result includes eligible, strictly earlier workbook rows for the same account and normalized merchant, the current learning library's source link and sheet/row pointers, and native applicable rules and their saved reasons. Native conflict, effective-date, deferred and withdrawn-rule behavior is unchanged. It also reads this draft's saved corrections and revision and any current accounting reason. Those draft records are separate from reusable rules.

Rules declare applicability to the selected account, merchant and date. Applicable policy sources also include their declared scope kind (`specific_accounts` or `all_owner_accounts`), current status/revision and up to ten saved status-change events with revision/time/source. Other account identifiers are omitted. Original declared scope and status history for other rule kinds, or sources no longer matching the selected scope, are explicitly unavailable. The legacy `cards` label field is omitted: empty display labels do not establish an unrestricted rule.

`selectedAccountOnly` distinguishes a policy restricted to this one account from a policy also covering other accounts or all owner accounts. It does not reveal those other account identifiers. When the native result reports conflicting selected rules, `ruleConflict` separately identifies the selected purchase and rule ids/count, the native reason and the absence of a native winner. Their account/merchant/date conditions already apply; unresolved priority does not mean those conditions are absent. A later status revision or historical majority is not a native tie-breaker. Non-conflict results return `ruleConflict: null`. Classification selection and values are unchanged by this descriptor.

Applied revision and source-read revision remain distinct because these reads are not an atomic snapshot. Saved status events can show that a previously deferred rule became active; a conflict in fresh evidence does not establish that an earlier answer was wrong. Missing or truncated status events do not establish a complete previous policy state or who changed it. Native learning decisions remain unchanged by this provenance read.

Examples are limited to 50 rows and 50 rules; corrections to the latest 20. Totals and truncation are declared. The native answer still uses the complete evidence. Prior classifications support a hypothesis; they do not establish the actual item, motive, consumption tax or invoice registration. An empty or unavailable history is not a personal/company decision. Non-expense rows return `applicable: false`. No draft, learning, approval or ledger writes occur.

This is purchase-specific retrieval, not full learning-library search or all-feature Navi completion. It does not grant bank access or repair legacy company permissions. Model reasoning and deployment verification are separate from the native API fixture.
